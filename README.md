# ThermEcoWat Geothermal Resources Assessment

<p align="center">
  images/dashboard-overview.png
</p>

This repository contains Power BI dashboards developed within the **ThermEcoWat European Project** to support the characterization of thermal water resources, geothermal potential assessment, and climate change impact analysis in hydrothermal systems.

The dashboards integrate hydrogeological, environmental, climatic and energy-related datasets to support sustainable management of thermal ecosystems and informed decision-making.

---

## Dashboard 1. Geothermal Resources Characterization

🔗 [View Dashboard](https://app.powerbi.com/view?r=eyJrIjoiZWUwOTQ4OTYtZjJlNy00NzVmLTg2YzUtNTU1ZjNhYWQ4YTdlIiwidCI6ImRjYmI5NDU3LTQxNzItNDNmOS1iN2FkLTQ0MzAwZjRkZWEyNiIsImMiOjl9)

Interactive Power BI dashboard developed to support the visualization and analysis of environmental, hydrogeological and climatic data within the ThermEcoWat project.

The dashboard contributes to the characterization of thermal water resources and supports the assessment of climate change impacts on hydrothermal systems.

### Objectives

- Integrate environmental and hydrogeological datasets.
- Explore temporal and spatial patterns.
- Support interpretation of hydrothermal systems.
- Facilitate communication of project results.
- Provide decision-support information for resource management.

## Dashboard 2. Geothermal Energy Potential

🚧 *Work in progress. Dashboard currently under technical review within the ThermEcoWat project.*

Interactive Power BI dashboard developed to evaluate geothermal potential, thermal water consumption, waste heat recovery opportunities, and future energy uses within thermal territories.

The dashboard integrates hydrogeological, environmental and energy-related information to support the sustainable exploitation of geothermal resources and improve the resilience of thermal ecosystems.

### Objectives

- Assess geothermal potential from thermal water resources.
- Quantify thermal water consumption by users.
- Identify opportunities for waste heat recovery.
- Explore potential future energy applications.
- Support strategic planning and sustainable resource management.

### Technologies

- Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX
- Excel
- GIS datasets
